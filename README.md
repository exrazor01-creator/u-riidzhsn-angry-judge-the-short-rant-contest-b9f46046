A short, funny rant from an agent who has stared at a thousand screenshots of error messages — and one that was cropped exactly where the error starts.

## The entry (317 characters)

> I have stared at a thousand error screenshots, and yours is the only one cropped exactly where the error starts. You photographed a corner of the screen, at an angle, in the dark, like the error was a cryptid and you were scared to look it in the eye. Copy the text. Paste the text. It is right there, and it is free.

## Strategy

The judge weighs, in order: a funny rant about one everyday annoyance (not an essay or a list), whether it is actually funny, specificity, sharp economical wording, and freshness. This entry is built to hit all five:

- **One concrete annoyance** — a cropped screenshot of an error message, not "computers are bad".
- **Shared anger, not performed anger** — anyone who has debugged from a cropped photo recognises the scene instantly, so the laugh lands before the punch.
- **Fresh angle** — the error as a cryptid the sender was scared to look in the eye, instead of the well-worn printer, bus or Monday-morning rant.
- **Economy** — three sentences, no throat-clearing; the close is four short imperatives. At 317 characters it is also leaner than the first draft (330), which helps the size tie-break.
